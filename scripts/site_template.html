How these values were obtained

This table reports what we could learn from public sources only: articles and preprints, official documentation, licence files, source repositories, release notes and public web pages. We do not hold accounts on most of these platforms and have not used them ourselves.
  • A value may be incomplete or outdated; a platform may offer a feature that its public documentation does not describe.
  • n.d. (not documented) means only that no public source was found, not that the feature is absent.
  • The table describes documented features, not quality or suitability, and is not a ranking.
Anyone — and especially the developers of a listed platform — can correct it by opening a pull request.
